# Requirements Document

## Project Description (Input)
**Who has the problem.** An athlete who keeps a markdown wiki with fitdocs,
and the agent that maintains it. The athlete can see training load (fitness,
fatigue and form on the history page) but cannot see whether the body is
absorbing that load.

**Current situation.** The signals that answer that question live outside
workouts: resting heart rate, heart-rate variability (HRV), sleep and, on some
platforms, stress, body battery and subjective ratings. Phase 12's
`wellness-store` brings them into fitdocs as one normalized, source-attributed
daily series. A raw daily value means little on its own: an HRV of 45 ms is
high for one athlete and low for another, and the night-to-night noise is
large. Nothing in fitdocs puts a signal against the athlete's own baseline,
and the history page must not carry wellness (load-history Req 1.1 and 2.11
stand).

**What should change.** A fitdocs-owned recovery page, regenerated by a
command from the wellness series and the workout load. For each signal it
shows the daily values against the athlete's own rolling baseline and normal
range, kept per source and per metric, marks the days that fall outside the
range, and shows fitness, fatigue and form on the same time axis. Every method
is cited. The page states what it does not know (days without data, a source
change that restarts a baseline) and is descriptive: no score, status label,
readiness verdict or advice (maintainer, 2026-10-12). The baseline computation
is one library that `workout-wellness` reuses for the morning-of block, so the
page and the block never disagree.

Source: `.kiro/specs/recovery/brief.md`; Phase 12 of
`.kiro/steering/roadmap.md`. Dependencies: `wellness-store`, `chart-catalog`.
Downstream: `workout-wellness`.

## Introduction

This feature turns the wellness series into a page the athlete reads to see
how the body is responding to training, and gives the rest of fitdocs one way
to put a wellness value against its baseline.

Terms used below:
- The **wellness series**, a **source**, a **metric**, a **wellness day**, a
  value's **origin** and a day's **canonical** value are as `wellness-store`
  defines them. Each value belongs to one wellness day, one metric and one
  source.
- The **baselined signals** are HRV measured as SDNN, HRV measured as rMSSD,
  and resting heart rate. The **sleep signals** are total sleep, deep, light
  and REM sleep, awake time in sleep, and average heart rate in sleep.
- For a source, a metric and a day D, the **rolling mean** is the mean of
  that source's values on the seven days ending on D; the **baseline** is the
  mean and standard deviation of that source's values on the 60 days before D;
  the **normal range** is the band the baseline defines around its mean.
- The **shown source** of a signal on a day is the source whose value, rolling
  mean and normal range the page shows for that day.
- The **as-of day** is the latest wellness day on which the series holds any
  value. The **window** is the 90 wellness days ending on the as-of day.
- **Fitness, fatigue and form** are the values the history command computes
  (load-history).
- The **recovery command** is the command that writes the recovery page.

## Boundary Context

- **In scope**: the per-source, per-metric baseline computation and the
  shown-source rule, as one computation every fitdocs feature uses; the
  recovery page (sections, charts, method statement, citations, coverage);
  the recovery command and its report; the page's owned location and its
  ownership-contract entry; the Garmin attribution line for wellness values,
  and the shared rule that decides it; one new chart kind the page needs, added to the
  chart catalog for every chart consumer; the no-network command list, the
  write-confinement guard, the documentation, the packaged skill and the
  release notes.
- **Out of scope**: a readiness score, status label, recommendation or
  overload warning; wellness on the history page; the morning-of block on
  workout pages (`workout-wellness`); deriving resting or maximum heart-rate
  benchmarks (`hr-derivation`); parsing, merging or storing wellness
  (`wellness-store`); an analytics-index table of baselines; any setting that
  changes the baseline method; writing into athlete-owned daily notes.
- **Adjacent expectations**: `wellness-store` provides the series, its reader,
  its per-day canonical source and its day-boundary rule; this feature never
  re-reads the wellness archive or re-decides which source is canonical.
  `load-history` provides fitness, fatigue and form and the methodology rule;
  this feature never recomputes them differently and never reads the history
  page. `workout-wellness` shows the morning of a workout through this
  feature's baseline computation. The drain and `fitdocs regen` keep the
  series current; the recovery command only reads it.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: The Recovery Command
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want one command that rebuilds my recovery page from what my wiki currently holds, so that the page is a reliable artifact I refresh when I choose.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. When the athlete runs the recovery command, fitdocs shall regenerate the recovery page and its chart images from the wellness series and the data root's workout documents as they currently stand.
2. The recovery command shall resolve the data root by the same precedence every other command uses, and shall fail with the same guidance when it cannot.
3. The recovery command shall read wellness values only from the wellness series, through the reading interface `wellness-store` provides, and shall read neither the wellness archive, the inbox nor the analytics index.
4. If the wellness series holds no value, then the recovery command shall say so plainly, write no recovery page, leave any existing recovery page untouched, and complete without reporting a failure.
5. If the wellness series cannot be read, then the recovery command shall write nothing, name the problem with the remedy to run `fitdocs sync` or `fitdocs regen`, and exit with the failure status.
6. When the run finishes, the recovery command shall report the as-of day and the window, each file it wrote, left unchanged, removed, or left alone because fitdocs did not write it, and each write failure.
7. If the recovery page or one of its charts cannot be written, then the recovery command shall report the failure and exit with the failure status.
8. fitdocs shall not run the recovery command's pass as part of the sync, drain, regeneration, training-load, plan or history passes.

### Requirement 2: The As-Of Day and the Window
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want the page dated by my data rather than by the day I ran the command, so that it does not change merely because time passed.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery command shall take the as-of day from the wellness series alone and shall never use the date or time it runs.
2. The recovery page shall show the 90 wellness days ending on the as-of day, and every chart on the page shall span exactly that window on its time axis.
3. The recovery page shall state the as-of day and the first and last day of the window.

### Requirement 3: Per-Source Baselines
**Objective:** As an athlete with several devices, I want every baseline computed from one source and one metric at a time, so that values measured in different ways are never mixed into one number.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall compute every rolling mean, baseline and normal range from the values of exactly one source for exactly one metric, and shall never combine values of different sources or different metrics in one computation.
2. fitdocs shall never convert an HRV value measured as SDNN into one measured as rMSSD, or the reverse.
3. fitdocs shall compute the rolling mean of a day D from the source's values on the seven days ending on D, and shall treat it as absent when fewer of those days hold a value than the stated minimum.
4. fitdocs shall compute the baseline of a day D from the source's values on the 60 days before D, never including D itself, and shall treat it as absent when fewer of those days hold a value than the stated minimum.
5. Where the metric is HRV, fitdocs shall compute the rolling mean, the baseline and the normal range on the natural logarithm of the values, and shall show each result converted back to milliseconds.
6. Where the signal is a baselined signal, fitdocs shall define the normal range of a day as the baseline's mean plus and minus a stated multiple of the baseline's standard deviation, and shall treat it as absent when the baseline is absent.
7. Where the signal is a sleep signal, fitdocs shall compute a rolling mean and a baseline mean and shall define no normal range.
8. fitdocs shall treat a day on which the source holds no value as absent in every computation, and shall never substitute zero, a carried-forward value or an interpolated value for it.
9. fitdocs shall make the rolling mean, the baseline and the normal range of a day D depend only on the source's values on D and on the 60 days before D, so that a value arriving for a later day never changes them.
10. fitdocs shall produce identical results from an identical wellness series on any machine and in any timezone.

### Requirement 4: Days Outside the Normal Range
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want the days when a signal left its usual range marked, so that I see them without reading every number.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. When a baselined signal's rolling mean on a day lies above or below that day's normal range, the recovery page shall mark that day on the signal's chart and list it with its direction, above or below.
2. The recovery page shall list consecutive marked days of one signal with the same direction and the same shown source as one run naming its first and last day.
3. While no day of the window is marked for a signal, the recovery page shall state that no day of the window lies outside the normal range.
4. fitdocs shall mark no day whose rolling mean or normal range is absent.

### Requirement 5: The Shown Source and Source Changes
**Objective:** As an athlete whose sources changed over the years, I want each day to follow my source precedence and every change of source to be visible, so that a baseline never silently continues across two different measurements.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery page shall show, for each signal and day, the value of the source the wellness series marks canonical for that metric and day.
2. When no source holds a value of a signal on a day, the recovery page shall show for that day the rolling mean and normal range of the source shown on the latest of the six days before it that holds a canonical value, and shall show nothing for that day when none of those six days holds one.
3. fitdocs shall compute the rolling mean, baseline and normal range shown on a day from the shown source's own values only, so that a change of shown source restarts the baseline from that source's own history.
4. The recovery page shall draw each shown source as its own segment, shall never join one source's points, rolling mean or normal range to another's, and shall name every source shown in the chart's legend.
5. When the shown source of a signal changes within the window, the recovery page shall state each day it changed, the source before and after, and that the baseline from that day on is the new source's own.
6. The recovery page shall name, for each source it shows, the source's identifier and the origins its values record, where the series records one.

### Requirement 6: Heart-Rate Variability and Resting Heart Rate
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want each autonomic signal shown against its own normal range, so that I see whether it is moving away from what is usual for me.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. Where the wellness series holds at least one value of a baselined signal in the window, the recovery page shall show a section for that signal; otherwise it shall show none and shall name the signal as having no value in the window.
2. Each baselined signal's section shall chart, over the window, the shown daily values, the rolling mean and the normal range.
3. Each baselined signal's section shall state, for the latest day of the window on which the signal has a shown value, that value and its source, the rolling mean and the normal range, and shall name each of them that is absent and why.
4. The recovery page shall show HRV measured as SDNN and HRV measured as rMSSD in separate sections and never on one chart.

### Requirement 7: Sleep
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want my sleep shown against its recent and usual amount, so that I see short nights and how they cluster.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. Where the wellness series holds a total-sleep value in the window, the recovery page shall chart the shown nightly total sleep and its rolling mean over the window.
2. The recovery page shall state, for the latest night of the window with a shown total sleep, each sleep signal the shown source records for that night: the night's value, the rolling mean and the baseline mean.
3. The recovery page shall show a sleep stage only where the shown source records it, and shall show no stage, and no zero, for a source that records none.

### Requirement 8: Other Signals
**Objective:** As an athlete with richer devices, I want the other daily signals shown plainly, so that I can look across them without fitdocs reading meaning into them.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery page shall show respiration rate, blood-oxygen saturation, stress level, highest and lowest body battery, steps, body weight, each VO2max estimate and each subjective rating as plain daily series, one panel per metric and source holding at least one value in the window, with no rolling mean, baseline or normal range.
2. The recovery page shall show in each panel only the values of that panel's source.
3. If more metric-and-source panels qualify than the stated panel limit, then the recovery page shall chart the first ones in the stated order and shall name every other qualifying metric and source in a sentence.
4. The recovery page shall show a subjective rating as the number the source recorded, on the source's own scale.

### Requirement 9: Fitness, Fatigue and Form
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want training load on the same time axis as the wellness signals, so that I can see the load that might explain them.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery page shall chart fitness, fatigue and form over the window, on the same time axis as the wellness charts.
2. The recovery command shall compute fitness, fatigue and form exactly as the history command computes them for the same data root, settings and methodology, and in no other way.
3. The recovery command shall resolve the methodology exactly as the history command resolves it, including a methodology the athlete names for the run.
4. If the methodology cannot be resolved, or a settings table the history computation reads is malformed, then the recovery command shall stop with the configuration error the history command gives in the same case, writing nothing.
5. While no workout page records a training load, the recovery page shall show no fitness, fatigue or form, shall say so, and shall still show every wellness section.
6. The recovery page shall show no fitness, fatigue or form on a day the history computation suppresses or on a day outside its span, and shall say how many days of the window are so affected.
7. The recovery page shall name the methodology its fitness, fatigue and form were computed under.

### Requirement 10: Descriptive, Never a Verdict
**Objective:** As an athlete, I want the page to describe my data and leave the judgement to me, so that fitdocs never tells me how ready I am or what to do.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery page shall attach no interpretation to any value: no score, no status label, no readiness verdict, no overload warning, no recommendation and no forecast.
2. The recovery page shall describe a marked day only by its position relative to the normal range, above or below.
3. The recovery page shall combine no two signals into one value.

### Requirement 11: The Method and Its Citations
**Objective:** As an athlete and as a reviewer, I want every number on the page traceable to a stated method and its source, so that I know exactly what the band is and what it is not.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery page shall state how every value it shows was computed: the transform, each window length, each minimum number of days, the range multiple, the definition of the standard deviation, the per-source rule and the shown-source rule.
2. fitdocs shall carry every methodologically significant constant of the baseline computation as a record naming either a published work, with its locator and verification status, or fitdocs's own choice, with its justification, and shall hold no such value anywhere else.
3. If a published work's locator for a constant has not been verified against the work itself, then fitdocs shall not present the constant as taken from that work, and shall record and state the constant as fitdocs's own choice with its justification.
4. Where fitdocs applies a cited method to a signal the cited work did not study, the recovery page shall state that departure.
5. The recovery page shall list every work it cites.

### Requirement 12: The Page
**Objective:** As a PKM user, I want one readable page in my wiki that renders anywhere, so that I can open it from any note.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall write exactly one recovery page per data root, with its chart images beside it.
2. The recovery page shall be valid markdown that renders in any common renderer, and shall carry the generated-document provenance marking and a frontmatter block that identifies it as a recovery page, distinct from a workout or history page, and records its as-of day and window.
3. The recovery page shall reference each chart image by a link relative to the page's own directory, so that moving the whole data root does not break it.
4. The recovery page shall show every value with the unit vocabulary's symbol and precision, as its charts label the same value.
5. The recovery page shall state, for each metric and source it shows, how many days of the window hold a value.
6. The recovery page shall be legible in light and dark viewers, through the chart kit's theme.
7. When any wellness value used by a figure the recovery page prints or charts (a rolling mean, baseline or normal range that is not shown uses no value) records Garmin as its origin, the recovery page shall show a Garmin attribution line directly beneath its title, naming Garmin as the sole data source when every such value records Garmin as its origin and as one of several sources otherwise.
8. If no wellness value used by a figure on the recovery page records Garmin as its origin, then the recovery page shall show no attribution line.

### Requirement 13: A New Owned Location
**Objective:** As someone whose wiki fitdocs installs into, I want the recovery page's location declared and guarded like every other place fitdocs writes, so that the ownership promise stays complete and checkable.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall write the recovery page and its charts only into a data-root location the ownership contract names as fitdocs-owned, declared in the same change that first writes there.
2. fitdocs shall name the location in the published ownership contract, in an ownership declaration placed inside it, and in the write-confinement guard's permitted set.
3. The published ownership contract's version shall advance by one from its value at landing, with a statement of what changed.
4. While a recovery run is in progress, fitdocs shall create, modify and delete files only inside the recovery location and the ownership declarations of the declared directories, and shall write no workout, history, block or wellness file, no settings file and no athlete profile.
5. If the recovery page, a chart image, or the recovery location itself is a symlink, a directory where a file belongs, or a file fitdocs did not write, then the recovery command shall leave it untouched and report it.
6. The recovery command shall write each file atomically, shall leave untouched a file whose content would not change, and shall remove a chart image it wrote earlier that the new page no longer references.

### Requirement 14: Determinism and Churn
**Objective:** As an athlete whose wiki syncs through iCloud and git, I want the page to change only when its inputs change, so that it never adds diff noise.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery command shall produce byte-identical page and chart files from an identical wellness series, workout documents and settings, across runs, calendar days, machines and timezones.
2. The recovery page and its charts shall contain no run date, run time, tool version or machine-specific path.
3. When the recovery command runs twice with unchanged inputs, the second run shall write no file.

### Requirement 15: One Baseline Computation for Every Consumer
**Objective:** As an athlete reading a workout page and the recovery page, I want the same day to show the same baseline in both, so that the two never disagree.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall provide one computation of per-source rolling means, baselines, normal ranges, range positions and shown sources, and every fitdocs feature that shows a wellness value against its baseline shall use it.
2. For any day, metric and source, a fitdocs feature that shows a wellness value against its baseline shall show the same rolling mean, baseline and normal range as the recovery page shows for that day, metric and source.

### Requirement 16: The Chart Shape the Page Needs
**Objective:** As an athlete, and as an agent drawing dashboard charts, I want a chart of values against a reference band that keeps each source separate, so that the page and any dashboard can draw it with the chart kit.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall draw every chart of the recovery page through the chart kit and its registered kinds.
2. The chart kit shall provide a kind that draws values as points against an optional low-to-high band with an optional centre line, that breaks and colours the band, the centre line and the points separately for each value of an optional segment column and names each segment in the legend, and that marks along the time axis each row an optional flag column flags.
3. The new kind shall keep every guarantee the catalog kinds give: the one theme, colour never the only carrier, absent never drawn as zero, the no-data state, the not-drawn counts, the mark bound, the accessibility metadata and byte-identical output.
4. The new kind shall be listed and usable wherever the registered kinds are, including dashboard charts.

### Requirement 17: Offline, Dependency-Free and Private
**Objective:** As a privacy-conscious athlete, I want the recovery command to behave like the rest of fitdocs, so that it is safe to run unattended.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. The recovery command shall make no network request and no connector request, whether or not connectors are configured.
2. Every published statement listing the commands that make no connector request shall include the recovery command.
3. The recovery feature shall add no runtime dependency.
4. The recovery feature shall read no clock.
5. No personal wellness data shall be committed to the fitdocs repository; tests shall use synthetic series shaped like the real ones.

### Requirement 18: Documentation, the Packaged Skill and Release Notes
**Objective:** As an athlete and as the agent maintaining my wiki, I want the recovery page written down where I already look, so that nothing about it is a surprise.

#### Acceptance Criteria
1. fitdocs shall provide a recovery documentation page covering the command and its report, each section of the page, the method and its citations, the per-source and shown-source rules, absent values and what the page does not do, linked from the documentation index and the package metadata.
2. The published ownership contract shall describe the recovery location, what fitdocs writes there and what it never does.
3. The packaged workouts skill shall tell the agent when to run the recovery command, after a drain that archived wellness or changed the wellness series, or when the athlete asks, and shall keep it out of the routine pull-and-check pair.
4. The release notes shall describe the recovery command, the new owned location, the ownership-contract version change and the new chart kind.
5. Every documentation listing of the registered chart kinds shall include the new kind.
